# ADR-005: Risk Engine before Execution

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Trading Lab executes decisions generated by rule-based strategies and agentic-AI strategies.

Without a mandatory risk validation step, a strategy or agent could produce invalid or unsafe decisions, such as:

- buying more than available cash allows
- selling without holding a position
- trading assets outside V1 scope
- exceeding max position size
- exceeding max trade frequency
- violating max drawdown limits
- attempting short selling
- attempting margin-like behavior

This is especially important for agentic-AI decisions, because LLM outputs may be malformed, overconfident, or inconsistent.

## Decision

Every `TradingDecision` must pass through the Risk Engine before execution.

No order may be created without a persisted `RiskCheck`.

The Risk Engine is authoritative over final executable action and position size.

## Rationale

This enforces a single safety boundary before execution.

The Risk Engine ensures:

- system constraints are enforced consistently
- agent suggestions cannot bypass hard rules
- execution logic receives validated instructions
- every order can be linked to a risk decision
- audit trails remain complete

## Alternatives Considered

### Trust strategies to self-validate

Rejected.

This would duplicate risk logic and allow inconsistent behavior across strategies.

### Trust agent reasoning

Rejected.

LLM-based reasoning is not a hard safety mechanism.

### Put risk checks inside the broker adapter

Rejected.

Broker adapters should handle broker communication, not domain-level risk policy.

## Consequences

### Positive

- centralized safety enforcement
- consistent behavior across strategies
- easier testing
- stronger auditability
- safer agentic-AI integration

### Negative

- every execution flow must include RiskCheck creation
- strategies may require additional context for risk validation
- more persistence records are created

## Implementation Rules

- Every TradingDecision must have a corresponding RiskCheck.
- Execution Module must not create an Order before RiskCheck exists.
- HOLD decisions usually do not create Orders.
- Rejected or adjusted decisions must be recorded in RiskCheck.
- Agent-suggested position size may be reduced or rejected.
- Risk Engine must enforce:
  - strategy/mode-specific asset allowlists in V1
  - no short selling
  - no margin trading
  - no selling without position
  - no buying without sufficient cash
  - max position size
  - max trades per day or week
  - max drawdown behavior
  - fallback HOLD on invalid decisions
